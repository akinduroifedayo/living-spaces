## Design specificity and before/after

**Verdict:** all three now have real authorship and distinct moods instead of generic kit skins, but the editorial layer sometimes consumes space that the task and data need. Keep observatory / warm field journal / orchard; do not re-theme. The most important refinement is to make the working information and next action as well composed as the decoration.

### Sky — quiet observatory
- **Observed:** baseline Today has large dark dashboard cards and shows Chicago/Lisbon details side by side, with Tokyo below fold. Rev 11 replaces that with three compact, selectable city summaries and one generous Chicago scene: ink, ivory serif, fine gold and a celestial illustration (`sky-opened` → `sky-desktop-today`). This improves city comparison and atmosphere, but puts much of the selected city's useful detail below the screenshot fold. Illustration is a large pale sun behind cloud even while the condition says **Overcast**; its decorative meaning is ambiguous rather than evidence of incorrect weather.
- **Observed:** baseline Week is a high/low line chart with rain bars and hover/touch hint. Rev 11's seven day columns show conditions, numeric lows/highs, shared-scale range marks, and rain percentages/totals without requiring chart hover (`sky-week` → `sky-desktop-week`). This is more immediately scannable, but 7 separate columns and small precipitation labels tax vision; the chart's more direct trend contour is lost. City management retains explicit latitude/longitude, favorite and removal; new inline `Confirm remove`/`Cancel` state is reassuring (`sky-cities`, `sky-desktop-remove-confirm`).
- **Phone observed:** 390px `sky-phone-390` stacks masthead, freshness, tabs, headline, three narrow city cards and the hero. Most detail starts below the crop. Long condition text wraps to several lines. Plus-only management affordance is ambiguous in isolation. **Hypothesis:** people checking weather on the move may scroll past ornamental introduction to reach actionable data.

### Research — warm editorial
- **Observed:** baseline overview is a dense, dark operations dashboard with library counts, status bars, and review CTA. Rev 18 becomes a cream journal: serif headline, restrained rust accent, feed and forest-green shortlist (`research-overview` → `research-desktop-overview`). It has a stronger reading identity, but the large slogan and editorial statement displace immediately usable counts; first screen prioritizes mood over the work queue. Baseline `research-queue` has a selected-item instruction panel and priority filter; after `research-desktop-queue` is captured deep in a long list with a large blank right region. It is not proof of an application bug, but that captured position gives little navigational context. Review after is also scrolled into the editor; baseline review puts paper position and actions together.
- **Observed:** the edited screenshot explicitly says “Unsaved draft · save before choosing another paper or view”; the saved screenshot says “✓ Saved”. Public demo provenance is stated alongside the source URL. This is a strong trust pattern; saved notes/status persistence and search are independently reported in functional evidence, not re-tested here. The visible 47-record/search changes are data-dependent, not aesthetic outcomes.
- **Phone observed:** `research-phone-390` is in Library with search `attention`; the add-paper form occupies the rest of the captured Space viewport, and results are below the captured portion. **Hypothesis:** placing add form between search and results interrupts the user's search-to-result path; verify full scroll and focus behavior before changing. No overflow was reported at 390px.

### Pair — orchard
- **Observed:** baseline is a narrow dark utility board with blue star backs; rev 10 widens it into a cream/forest editorial table with numbered, patterned cards and a separate round/progress rail (`pair-play` → `pair-desktop-resume`). The pattern and fine rules are distinctive, but the found fruit are native emoji, not an integrated illustrated fruit set: their platform-dependent glossy rendering sits apart from the carefully drawn card backs. No new asset call is needed: simplify treatment or harmonize the surrounding face with the existing emoji, without implying an illustration exists.
- **Observed:** mismatch state shows both fruits, dashed borders, “Not quite. Remember these two” and `Continue →`; completion shows all six pairs and `Play again`. Functional evidence says mismatch is intentionally held until Continue and restored after interruption. This respects attention but adds a step each mismatch; do not remove without validating timing and accessibility. Progress after is more spacious than the baseline and gives explicit six-of-six and turn totals.
- **Observed:** `pair-desktop-history` shows `643m 59s` for the resumed round. README states this is wall-clock time since the original start; it is accurate elapsed time, not demonstrated active play time. Scratch history source uses finish minus start, consistent with that explanation. A duration without qualification can mislead. Phone capture shows the four-column board after a large four-line headline; top of board is visible but lower cards and completion action extend beyond the crop. Scrolling is not itself clipping or a failure.

## Strengths
1. Sky's seven-day ribbon exposes actual values and labels without the baseline chart's hover dependency; saved-city selection and confirmation have clear visual affordances.
2. Research's source/demo provenance and explicit unsaved/saved states support trust at the precise point of editing; the journal identity makes papers feel like reading material rather than admin rows.
3. Pair's mismatch is legible through text and dashed outlines, not just color, while persistent progress and a clear completion action create a gentle ending.

## Priority defects and exact fixes

1. **P1 — Research search path is visually interrupted on phone (observed ordering; effect hypothesis).** The captured Library phone places a new-paper form directly after the search and before any visible result. Move results/count immediately below search; put “Add a paper” in a collapsed secondary disclosure after results or in a clearly labeled secondary action. Keep keyboard focus and entered data intact on open/close. Verify at 390px with matches, zero matches, long titles and keyboard; do not assume the unseen result list is broken. `/impeccable layout`.
2. **P1 — Core information is pushed down by promotional headings (observed framing; task impact hypothesis).** In Sky phone, a multi-line slogan precedes the current-city details; Pair phone spends four lines on the slogan before the board; Research desktop gives substantial area to its slogan above first feed item. At ≤390px cap headings to two lines or use compact type and spacing; in Sky show selected city temperature/condition before atmospheric scene; in Pair place the board after a concise status; Research keep one editorial line and move shortlist/count/first actionable paper higher. Preserve each identity and verify actual viewport with large text and no loss of controls. `/impeccable adapt`.
3. **P2 — Pair elapsed duration implies a play-time metric (observed).** Replace `643m 59s` with “Elapsed 10h 43m · includes time away” (seconds optional), derived from the existing timestamps; do not label it active play unless active time is separately recorded. Preserve historic records and show accessible full wording. `/impeccable clarify`.
4. **P2 — Small, muted secondary data and ambiguous mobile action (observed visual risk; compliance unmeasured).** Increase Sky forecast rain/date text, Pair fine print/fruit labels and Research metadata to a comfortably readable minimum; increase contrast after measuring rendered foreground/background, and give Sky's mobile plus a visible “Manage cities” label. Check WCAG contrast and at least 44×44 CSS-pixel touch targets by instrumented inspection, not by screenshot inference. `/impeccable audit`.
5. **P2 — High-stakes absent states are not evidenced (coverage gap, not a confirmed defect).** Capture and test Sky offline/stale/partial fetch and retry retaining last-known timestamp; Research refresh/save failure with preserved draft and actionable retry, plus empty search/queue; Pair load/save failure and empty history with return-to-play action. Respect reduced motion, avoid disorienting flips, and announce loading/success/error politely or assertively as appropriate. Implement only gaps confirmed by these tests. `/impeccable harden`.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey

Checklist is a visual assessment, not a task experiment. **Sky: moderate provisional** — single focus and hierarchy weakened by scenery before details; Week deliberately exposes seven day options/data columns (>4), but dates are one coherent time series, not seven simultaneous mandatory decisions. Grouping and numeric labels help; memory across screens untested. **Research: moderate provisional** — search/add/results compete and the overview's slogan competes with work; grouped queue and native selections help; result count and scroll context need verification. Four top-level tabs are visible; >4 paper rows are a browse list, not inherently >4 simultaneous choices. **Pair: moderate intrinsic task load** — 12 cards are intentionally simultaneous memory-game options (>4); collapsing the board would damage the game. Its repeated state counters and very large headline add avoidable extraneous load, while names, numbers and matched indicators assist recognition. Working-memory demands are inherent, not evidence of poor usability. Checklist categories not observable in captures (retention across navigation and progressive disclosure behavior) remain unscored.

Sky starts calm and informative, but cold/offline failure reassurance is unknown. Research has an attractive arrival and a strong saved confirmation, but first meaningful action is less immediate; unsaved warning is reassuring. Pair's mismatch is non-punitive and the end celebrates completion; the long wall-clock duration can spoil that ending. Persona risks: **Jordan**, new to Sky, may not infer plus = city management; **Sam**, low vision, may struggle with faint miniature metadata, with screen-reader and actual contrast unknown; **Casey**, interrupted on a phone, must scroll past long headings, while persistence is evidenced only for the specific reported host checks.
